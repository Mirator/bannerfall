# Bannerfall — Casual Playtester Session Diary

**Tester profile:** casual/indie player (Stardew, match-3, Kingdom Two Crowns on easy). No Thronefall/Bannerlord experience. Doesn't read manuals, learns by poking. Frustrated by unclear UI and unfair deaths; quits games that waste time.

Session length: ~10-12 sim minutes of active play (plus some standing-around time).

---

## Session Diary

**[00:00] Title screen.** "BANNERFALL — Raise a warband. Raze the camps. Take Wolfsjaw Hold." Press ENTER to ride. There's a control-scheme block at the bottom (WASD/mouse/space, troop orders 1/2/3, recruiting tips). Honestly I skimmed it and only half-registered it — I never read tutorial text carefully, I just want to get going. Filed it away as "there's a cheat sheet if I get lost."

**[00:05] Dropped into the world.** Gold 80, troops 4/12, HP 120/120. Objective banner: "Raze the bandit camps (0/3) to unlock Wolfsjaw." Clear enough goal. I see a village (Ashford), mountains, a river, and two enemy clusters with numbers over their heads across the river.

**[00:20] First confusion.** There's a floating number next to my own banner (varies: 5, 6, 7, 8 across the session) that never seemed tied to my troop count in the top-left HUD. I never figured out what it meant — no tooltip, nothing. Low-grade nagging confusion the whole session.

**[00:30–01:30] First real fight, unannounced.** I just moved around, and suddenly the screen changed entirely — new zoomed-in arena, a health bar and "1 FOLLOW / 2 CHARGE / 3 HOLD" buttons, night lighting, "Warband 2 · Slain 3/5" in the corner. I hadn't consciously "attacked" anything — I'd apparently walked into a bandit's aggro range while just wandering, and by the time I saw the screen, the fight was already 3/5 done and my health/troops had already been spent. That's disorienting — I want to know a fight is starting before it's half over.

**[01:30] Ashford is a recruiting hub — nice and clear.** Standing near it pops a panel: "Q Spearman 15g · E Archer 25g · F Rest & heal 10g." This was the single clearest, most satisfying UI moment all session — press a letter, watch gold drop and the stat go up immediately. Rest & heal took me from 60/120 to full for 10g, visibly. I recruited freely here multiple times over the session.

**[02:00–04:00] The river problem.** I picked a fight with a camp across the river. My troops crossed and started fighting; I tried to follow (held D toward the far bank) and just... didn't move across. Held it again, longer — still stuck at the bank. Meanwhile my troops, alone on the far side, got shot/cut down one by one while I stood uselessly on the near bank. Watched my warband go from 3 to 2 to 0 without being able to do anything about it. My own HP also drained (green bar went red) with no clear on-screen cause — I wasn't in visible melee with anything. This felt like the definition of an "unfair death": lost troops to a fight I structurally couldn't participate in.

**[04:00] Retreat.** A hint appeared: "← ride west: RETREAT." Rode away. Result: back on the overworld, 0/3 quest progress unchanged, troops and gold both down. So the failed river fight cost real resources for literally nothing — no partial credit, no consolation.

**[04:30–06:00] Healed, recruited, tried a camp that had no river in the way** (further wandering) — my troops charged ahead on "2 CHARGE" and were wiped out before I even reached them. This happened almost instantly after the order. Chasing them down and fighting solo eventually ended the skirmish and dumped me back at Ashford — but the quest counter was still 0/3. So apparently not every fight against red-shirted bandits counts as one of "the 3 camps" — there seem to be generic wandering skirmishers as well as the "real" camps, and nothing on screen distinguishes them in advance. You find out after the fact whether it "counted."

**[06:00–08:00] Two genuine wins.** Two separate skirmishes ended in a proper "VICTORY" banner: "+30 gold · 4 slain · 1 of your men fell" and later "+30 gold · 4 slain · no losses." These felt great — clean, legible, rewarding. The second one (no losses) was the high point of the whole session.

**[08:00] Still 0/3 after two victories.** Confirmed: those "VICTORY" skirmishes were not the bandit camps the main quest wants razed. At this point I genuinely didn't know what a "camp" looks like on the map versus a random patrol — both show as a red figure with a number over its head.

**[08:30–11:00] The big fight, and the stuck 6/7.** Wandered into a 7-enemy night encounter — "Warband 3/1 · Slain 2/7" climbing quickly to 6/7 as I helped. Then nothing. No sixth kill happened for over a minute of in-game time even after multiple direct sword clicks on what looked like the last standing figure near my banner. In hindsight I think I was repeatedly swinging at my *own* ally trooper (rendered in similar light-blue/white) instead of the actual last bandit, who I never located. Health bar stayed full, nothing progressed, "Slain 6/7" never ticked to 7/7. I waited, moved around, clicked more — completely stuck. Had to ride west and eat the loss to get out.

**[11:00] Session end.** Final stats: 20 gold, 1/12 troops, 56/120 HP, quest still "0/3." After ~10+ minutes of fighting, multiple wins, and one large battle, I have nothing to show for it on the one counter the game told me mattered.

---

## Confusion list
1. Floating number next to my own banner (5–8) — never understood what it tracked; not troop count, no tooltip.
2. Combat starts with no warning — you're already mid-fight (sometimes already losing) before you register the mode switch.
3. Some enemy encounters are "real camps" (count toward 0/3) and some are just wandering skirmishes (don't count) — visually identical on the map, only distinguishable after the fight resolves.
4. Rivers can silently block your own movement while your troops fight on the far bank — no visible indicator (ford, bridge, "can't cross here" message) telling you why you're stuck at the bank.
5. Ally troops and enemy troops are hard to tell apart at a glance in the heat of battle (both rendered as small pale humanoid sprites) — I spent real time swinging at what I now think was my own soldier.
6. The last-enemy stall in the 7-enemy fight: "Slain 6/7" simply never resolved, no visible remaining target, no timeout, no way to end it besides eating a resource-losing retreat. Reads as a soft-lock.

## Delight list
1. Ashford's recruit panel — instant, legible cause-and-effect (press letter, gold drops, stat goes up). Best UI moment in the game.
2. Rest & Heal for 10g — cheap, clear, instantly visible full-health bar.
3. The two clean "VICTORY" banners with a one-line breakdown (+gold, slain count, losses) — satisfying and easy to read.
4. A "no losses" victory felt genuinely good, like I'd played it right that time.

## Rage list
1. Losing an entire warband to enemies I structurally could not reach because of the river, with no way to intervene.
2. Troops instantly dying on "CHARGE" order before I could catch up or help.
3. The 6/7-forever stall — burned several minutes of real effort with zero payoff and no way to end it cleanly.
4. Multiple full fights (with real troop/gold cost) that turned out not to count toward the only stated goal (0/3 camps), discovered only after the fact.

## Quit-or-hooked verdict
**Quit.** The flip happened at the 6/7 stall (~session minute 10-11) — that's where "this game is a bit rough but I'm making progress" turned into "I have spent ten minutes, lost almost my whole warband, and the one number the game told me to watch (0/3) hasn't moved once." A casual player in my persona does not have the patience to debug a possible soft-lock or figure out camp-vs-skirmish distinctions on their own. I'd close the tab here.

## Would I recommend to a friend like me?
No, not in this state. I'd tell a Stardew/match-3 friend "the recruiting-and-healing loop is genuinely cute, but the combat has some rough unfair-death edges (rivers, ally/enemy mixups) and I never actually made progress on the main goal after a real chunk of playtime — wait for a patch."

## Fun score: 4/10
The bones are likeable — riding around, a real village with clear priced actions, a couple of crunchy clean victories with satisfying banners. But the core loop (raze 3 camps) never paid off once in the whole session despite multiple fights and two outright wins, I lost a full warband to a mechanic (the river) I couldn't even engage with, and I hit what felt like a genuine stuck state in the biggest fight of the run. A casual player forgives one bad death; getting nowhere on the stated goal after real effort, on top of a possible soft-lock, is where I tap out.

## One-line summary
**Fun score:** 4/10 · **Quit** (at the 6/7-forever stall, after ~10-11 min) · **Top confusion:** no way to tell a "real" bandit camp (counts toward 0/3) from a random skirmish (doesn't) until after the fight · **Top delight:** Ashford's instant, legible recruit/heal panel (press a letter, see gold drop and stat rise immediately).
